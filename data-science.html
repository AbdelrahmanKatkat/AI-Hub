How to learn from this

Every lesson starts with a decision someone must make and the data that could inform it. Then comes a hand-worked example on a 5–10 row table, the formal definition, an animated lab you can edit, traps, real uses and self-check questions. Samples arrive one by one so you can watch estimates settle and uncertainty shrink.

The maths and ML are linked, not repeated: distributions and estimation point to Mathematics, and models and evaluation metrics to Machine Learning.

The data science loop

Every data science project walks these six stages, and loops back when a result raises a new question. Click a stage for what it does, what it needs and where its data is stored.

The map

Module 0 frames the problem: questions, data types and tidy tables. Module 1 wrangles and cleans, and Module 2 explores. Module 3 supplies the statistics: sampling, confidence intervals, tests and the bootstrap. Module 4 turns results into honest charts, and Module 5 covers experiments and causal inference. Module 6 builds features and pipelines without leakage, Module 7 covers predictive modelling in practice, and Module 8 adds time series and forecasting. Module 9 communicates, monitors and keeps the work responsible.
